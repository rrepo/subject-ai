\noindent Dear Editor,

\vspace{1em}

I am writing to submit our manuscript titled \textbf{"Does Anonymity Inflate the Subject? A Comparative LLM-Based Analysis of Political and 4chan Discourse"} for consideration in \textit{Discover Artificial Intelligence}. 

This manuscript is being submitted as a \textbf{journal transfer} following a recommendation from the \textit{Journal of Intelligent Information Systems}. We are excited to take advantage of the transfer offer and believe our work aligns perfectly with the scope of \textit{Discover AI}, particularly in its application of advanced AI models to social science and linguistic analysis.

Our study investigates whether anonymity encourages an inflated, collective sense of authority—a phenomenon we term "subject size." To address this, we developed a novel methodology utilizing a Large Language Model (\texttt{Mistral-Nemo-12B}) to quantify subject abstraction across approximately 10,000 digital posts. By comparing the discourse of identified US politicians with that of anonymous 4chan users, we provide empirical evidence regarding the "online disinhibition effect."

Key highlights of our work relevant to \textit{Discover Artificial Intelligence} include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{AI-Driven Methodology:} Leveraging \texttt{Mistral-Nemo-12B} to automate complex linguistic classification at scale.
    \item \textbf{Interdisciplinary Insight:} Demonstrating how LLMs can be used as a robust tool for quantitative analysis in computational linguistics and social psychology.
    \item \textbf{Open Science:} In accordance with your journal's principles, we have made our full source code and datasets publicly available on GitHub to ensure transparency and reproducibility.
\end{itemize}

I confirm that this manuscript is original and not currently under consideration elsewhere. We have no conflicts of interest to disclose.

Thank you for your time and for the opportunity to transfer our work to \textit{Discover Artificial Intelligence}. We look forward to your positive response.
